# Current sensing

Use a four-terminal low-ohm shunt with Kelvin traces. For a 2 mΩ shunt, 25 A produces 50 mV and 1.25 W; 40 A produces 80 mV and 3.2 W. Amplifier gain and threshold remain configurable until the motor waveform and shunt pulse rating are measured. Firmware limiting never replaces the external fuse.
