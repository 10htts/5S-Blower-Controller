# Copper and wiring

The current PCB uses wide power zones on both layers, stitching vias, a compact switching loop, and Kelvin shunt traces. KiCad DRC confirms geometric connectivity and configured clearances; it does not establish ampacity or temperature rise.

Two-ounce copper is the provisional fabrication target, not a validated minimum. Confirm finished copper thickness with the fabricator and calculate/measure copper and via temperature rise from the actual continuous and transient motor current. Validate solder volume, pad geometry, connector and wire gauge, lead length, voltage drop, strain relief, enclosure clearances, and external fuse placement before release.
